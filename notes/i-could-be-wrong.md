# I Could Be Wrong

A persistent ledger of doubts. The opposite of confidence theatre.

For each topic you are studying, this file holds **the most likely ways your current understanding is wrong** — written as if you were briefing a hostile reviewer.

`/es-iwrong` adds entries. Entries are not deleted; they are resolved (with a pointer to evidence) or aged (with a note that the doubt has hardened into a known limitation).

---

## Format per entry

```markdown
## <Topic> — <YYYY-MM-DD>

- **Most likely error:** <one sentence>
- **Why I might be wrong:** <2–3 sentences; the strongest version of the doubt>
- **What would resolve this:** <a concrete check, source, calculation, or experiment>
- **Status:** open | resolved-correct | resolved-wrong | aged-into-limitation
- **Resolution:** <when status changes, link to the evidence>
```
